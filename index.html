Why de-soot? ▾
Running on low gears (Lvl 1–3) or Thermostat idle leaves unburnt carbon soot on the glow-plug screen, which can cause white smoke or E-08 / E-10 startup failures. Running at Max (Lvl 10) for 15 minutes every ~20 low-idle hours bakes the chamber clean!

🎛️ GEAR vs. THERMO vs. FAN / VENT

  • GEAR Mode: Locks the fuel pump to a fixed heat output (Lvl 1–10). Best for predictable morning pre-heats in uninsulated trailers.
  • THERMO Mode: Targets a set room temp (8°C–36°C / 46°F–97°F). Once reached, the heater idles down to Lvl 1 rather than shutting completely off.
  • FAN / VENT: Runs only the blower fan with zero fuel and zero heat. Use it to air out fumes or circulate air in warm weather.

❄️ Cooldown Lockout & 12V Battery

  • Cooldown Cycle (3–5 mins): After tapping STOP, the blower fan stays on until the Heat Exchanger cools below ~55°C (131°F). Restart commands are ignored by the heater until cooldown finishes!
  • ⚠️ NEVER Cut 12V Power While Hot: Cutting battery power at 190°C+ (375°F+) stops the cooling fan and can melt the housing or fry the ECU. Overheat cutoff (E-05) triggers at 230°C–270°C (446°F–518°F).
  • CHARGE SOON Pill: Glow-plug startup pulls 10A–12A. Plug in your battery maintainer if resting voltage drops to ≤12.0V so ignition load doesn't trip an E-02 fault.
