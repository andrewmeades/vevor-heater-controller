Why de-soot? ▾
Running on low gears (H-1 / H-2) or Thermostat idle leaves unburnt carbon soot on the glow-plug screen, which can cause white smoke or E-08 / E-10 startup failures. Running at Max Output for 15 minutes every ~20 low-idle hours bakes the chamber clean!

⏰ Weekly Schedule & Reading the LCD Clock Icons

  • Collision-Proof Sync: Tapping Save Schedule pauses background polling, syncs the real-time clock, and double-writes the 40-byte timer table in 20-byte BLE chunks so updates never drop.
  • Startup Output Level: Scheduled starts fire up at your last active Mode & Gear (shown as Saved: H-X when OFF).
  • Alarm Bell (⏰) vs. Clock (🕒) on LCD: The icon with two bell ears on top (⏰) lights up whenever any timer day is armed, and turns OFF when you tap 🚫 CLEAR & DISABLE ALL TIMERS. A plain circle clock (🕒) just means the red digits are displaying the time of day.

⛽ Live + Offline Fuel Tank Tracking (CY5004)

  • Tap the Diesel Tank card whenever you top up or glance at the sight glass (CY5004 is 5.0 L / 1.32 gal with marks at 100%, 70%, 50%, 20%).
  • Automatic Offline Timer Deduction: Even if your phone is off or inside the house during a 5:00 AM hardware-timer run, the app automatically deducts elapsed scheduled burn the next time you open it—without double-counting live Bluetooth sessions!
  • 6-Level Dosing Curve: H-1 = 0.16 L/h (0.04 gal/h), H-2 = 0.23 L/h, H-3 = 0.30 L/h, H-4 = 0.38 L/h, H-5 = 0.45 L/h, up to H-6 (Max) = 0.52 L/h (0.14 gal/h).

🔋 Voltage, CO Monitor & Cooldown Lockout

  • Supply Voltage: CY5004 boards report whole integer volts over BLE—a full 12.8V battery on a NOCO float maintainer reads 12 V (BATT OK), active bulk charging reads 13 V–14 V (CHARGING), and ≤11 V (LOW BATT!) means charge soon before 11A glow-plug startup trips E-02.
  • Carbon Monoxide (CO): Live CO xx PPM shows on the unit's physical LED screen while BLE monitors the E-CO auto-shutoff alarm. Always keep a standalone battery CO detector in enclosed spaces.
  • ❄️ Cooldown Lockout (3–5 mins): After STOP, the fan purges heat until the Heat Exchanger drops below ~55°C (131°F). NEVER cut 12V battery power while hot (190°C+ / 375°F+)!
