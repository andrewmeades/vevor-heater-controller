Why de-soot? ▾
Running on low gears (Lvl 1–3) or Thermostat idle leaves unburnt carbon soot on the glow-plug screen, which can cause white smoke or E-08 / E-10 startup failures. Running at Max (Lvl 10) for 15 minutes every ~20 low-idle hours bakes the chamber clean!

⏰ Reliable Hardware Scheduling & Clearing

  • Long schedule packets (40 bytes) are automatically split into 20-byte Bluetooth Low Energy chunks so your heater never misses an update.
  • Want to be 100% sure the heater won't wake up tomorrow? Tap 🚫 CLEAR & DISABLE ALL TIMERS ON HEATER on the Schedule tab—it zeroes out every timer byte on the ECU.
  • Tap 🔍 CHECK SCHEDULE while connected to query the ECU's active timer table (or verify the last synced timestamp if your board revision uses write-only timer memory).

🛡️ Carbon Monoxide (CO) & Safety Cutoffs

  • CO Monitor: Displays live CO PPM if broadcast by your heater's BLE sensor, or monitors for hardware CO cutoff (Fault E-CO), which automatically kills fuel and purges the chamber. Always keep a standalone battery CO detector in enclosed sleeping spaces as a secondary backup.
  • Cooldown Lockout (3–5 mins): After tapping STOP, the fan stays on until the Heat Exchanger drops below ~55°C (131°F). Never cut 12V battery power while hot (190°C+ / 375°F+)!

🎛️ GEAR vs. THERMO vs. FAN / VENT

  • GEAR Mode: Fixed fuel pump speed (Lvl 1 = 0.16 L/h / 0.04 gal/h up to Lvl 10 = 0.52 L/h / 0.14 gal/h).
  • THERMO Mode: Targets 8°C–36°C (46°F–97°F) and throttles down to Lvl 1 idle once reached.
  • FAN / VENT: Runs only the blower fan with zero fuel and zero heat.
